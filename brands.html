Building a Unified
Brand Ecosystem

Vepuri Agro Products is building a focused brand ecosystem across coconut-based food, wellness, and emerging product categories. Every brand is rooted in creating value from coconut at every level.

Bulk & Industrial

Coasta Naturals

Role: Bulk product brand focused on high-quality coconut-based ingredients for industrial supply chains.

Coasta Naturals represents the industrial backbone of Vepuri, delivering consistency and scale to B2B partners worldwide. We specialize in high-purity coconut extracts and powders designed for large-scale food production.

Farm-Level Integration

Vepuri Farms & Nurseries

Role: Farm-level integration brand focused on supplying high-quality coconut saplings and building long-term farmer partnerships through a structured sourcing and buy-back model.

Vepuri Farms & Nurseries strengthens the foundation of the coconut ecosystem by enabling reliable plantation development. With certified saplings, large-scale nursery capacity, and a buy-back approach, we connect farmers directly to Vepuri’s processing network, ensuring consistency from farm to factory.
