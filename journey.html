Vepuri Growth Path

Journey Timeline

From a single idea to building a structured coconut ecosystem, Vepuri's journey is driven by one clear focus creating real value for farmers through processing and innovation. What started as a vision has grown into a scalable model connecting farms, factories, and markets.

2018

Coasta Naturals Launch

Introduced as Vepuri's first step into structured coconut value addition, focused on market-ready products with defined quality standards.

Regional Launch
2019

Manufacturing Initiation

Established the first coconut processing facility at Mallavalli, creating a foundation for controlled production and scalable operations.

Mallavalli Unit I
2020

Innovation Breakthrough

Pioneered India's first frozen grated coconut, expanding beyond traditional formats and enabling wider market access.

National Market
2023

Capacity Expansion

Commisioned second manufacturing unit at Mallavalli to strengthen production capabilities and support increasing demand.

Mallavalli Unit II
2025

India’s 1'st coconut cafe

Entered the consumer space with the launch of CocoMunzzz in Vijayawada, introducing coconut-based desserts and beverages.

Vijayawada
2026

Café Expansion

The CocoMunzzz concept expanded to Guntur, strengthening retail presence and direct connection with consumers.

Guntur
2026

Advanced Processing Facility

Commissioned a next-generation processing unit at Mallavalli, integrating advanced systems for automation and scale.
